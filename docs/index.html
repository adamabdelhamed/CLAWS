CLAWS

CLAWS is a real-time battle simulator that puts you in charge of large-scale combat. Direct units from above, jump into a fighter on the front lines, and drive every turning point yourself.

Rendered entirely in a retro command-line style, every battle plays out live in text and color, turning fast tactical decisions into cinematic chaos.
